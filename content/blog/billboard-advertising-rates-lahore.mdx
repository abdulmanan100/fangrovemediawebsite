Most people asking what a billboard costs in Lahore are really asking a different question: *why do two boards on the same road carry different rates?* The answer is that a rate is not a property of a road. It is the sum of a handful of things about one specific face, and every one of them can be checked before you ask for a quote.

This guide walks through those factors as Fangrove Media prices its own estate of <Stat name="totalSites" /> sites across Lahore, and explains what to send so a rate can come back the same day.

## Why is audience the biggest factor?

Because it is what you are actually renting. A billboard's job is to be seen by enough of the right people, often enough, and the daily audience figure is the closest single number to that.

## How do size and lighting change the price?

**Size** affects two lines on the bill: the site rent, and the cost of the flex printed for it. Faces on the network run from small street-level formats up to the <Stat name="largestFace" /> board at <Stat name="largestFaceSite" />. Larger faces carry larger rents, and the [flex print](/services/flex-printing) scales with the area.

**Lighting** decides how many hours a day you are buying. Lahore's winter evenings are long, and a non-lit face stops working when the light goes, which is right in the middle of the evening commute. Front-lit and back-lit faces keep working after dark, and the rate reflects that. Almost every billboard on the network is lit for this reason.

## How are billboards booked in Lahore?

Static sites are booked by the month, in cycles that start on the **1st or the 16th**. A rate is quoted per cycle and depends on how many cycles you take, so one month and six months are priced differently. The full process, from enquiry to proof that the board went up, is covered in [how to book a billboard in Lahore](/blog/how-to-book-a-billboard-lahore).

## Are digital screens priced differently?

Yes. On a [digital (SMD) screen](/services/dooh) you are not renting a face; you are buying slots in a loop that is shared with other advertisers. On Fangrove Media's screens the loop is <Spec service="dooh" label="Loop" />, with slots of <Spec service="dooh" label="Slot length" />.

What matters there is share of voice: how many advertisers are in the loop with you, and how long your slot is. Those are stated up front, along with the cost per thousand impressions, before you sign. [DOOH vs static billboards](/blog/dooh-vs-static-billboards) compares the two in more detail.
