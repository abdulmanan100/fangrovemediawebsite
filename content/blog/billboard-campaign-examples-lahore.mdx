The best way to judge billboard advertising is to look at real campaigns on real roads. These are campaigns that ran on Fangrove Media's boards and screens in Lahore, for retailers, a mall, an insurer, an education body, a car company, a solar company and a perfumer. Each is shown as the creative appeared on the face.

What follows describes only what the photographs and the campaign records show: the client, the location and the format. Briefs and results belong to the clients, and are not ours to publish.

## Retail: Carrefour, Al Fatah and Condis by Spain

![Carrefour express-delivery creative running on a Fangrove billboard in Lahore](/media/work/carrefour-ooh.webp)

**Carrefour** used a billboard to advertise express delivery. A service message on a billboard works when it is a single promise that can be read at a glance, and a delivery offer is exactly that.

![Al Fatah Superstore creative on the Wapda Town roundabout board, Lahore](/media/work/al-fateh-ooh.webp)

**Al Fatah Superstore** ran its creative on the Wapda Town roundabout board. Roundabouts are among the strongest billboard positions in Lahore, because slowing traffic gives a face a full read rather than a glance.

![Condis by Spain creative on the Hussain Chowk LED screen, Gulberg, Lahore](/media/work/condis-ooh.webp)

**Condis by Spain** combined outdoor with the Hussain Chowk LED screen in Gulberg, the flagship digital site on the network. A screen lets a retailer rotate offers through the same slot, which suits stores with changing promotions. [SMD screen advertising in Lahore](/blog/smd-screen-advertising-lahore) explains how.

## A mall: Packages Mall and the Packages Shopping Festival

![Packages Shopping Festival creative on a large-format board at Johar Town, opposite the Expo Centre, Lahore](/media/work/packages-shopping-festival.webp)

The **Packages Shopping Festival** (2020) ran on a large-format board at Johar Town, opposite the Expo Centre, and on digital as well as static faces. A dated event is the classic case for outdoor: the campaign has a fixed end, and every face points at it.

![Packages Mall creative on a digital streamer along Jam-e-Shireen Road, Lahore](/media/work/packages-mall-ooh.webp)

**Packages Mall** also ran outside the festival, including on a digital streamer along Jam-e-Shireen Road. Streamers are usually sold as a run along a stretch of road, so one trip can pass the message more than once.

## Financial services and education: IGI Life and ACCA

![IGI Life Vitality creative on the DHA Main Boulevard hoarding, Lahore](/media/work/igi-life-ooh.webp)

**IGI Life** placed its Vitality creative on the DHA Main Boulevard hoarding. Insurance is a long decision, and a board on a high-income residential corridor keeps the brand in front of the same households for weeks.

![ACCA creative on a portrait board along Model Town Link Road, Lahore](/media/work/acca-ooh.webp)

**ACCA** used a portrait board along Model Town Link Road. Portrait faces suit a tall, stacked layout; the design has to be built for the face rather than adapted from a landscape file. [How to design a billboard that reads from the road](/blog/billboard-design-tips) covers why.

## Automotive, energy and fragrance: Car First, Zero Carbon Solar and Asghar Ali Perfumes

![Car First creative on a central-median board along Model Town Link Road, Lahore](/media/work/car-first-ooh.webp)

**Car First** ran on a central-median board along Model Town Link Road. A board in the central median sits in the middle of the carriageway, in front of the traffic approaching it rather than off to one side.

![Zero Carbon Solar "Zero Electricity Bills" creative running on a Fangrove board in Lahore](/media/work/zero-carbon-solar.webp)

**Zero Carbon Solar** ran "Zero Electricity Bills": three words that carry the whole offer. It is a good example of the billboard rule that one idea, stated plainly, beats a list of features.

![Asghar Ali Perfumes creative on the Liberty hoarding, Lahore](/media/work/asghar-ali-perfumes.webp)

**Asghar Ali Perfumes** used the Liberty hoarding, in one of Lahore's busiest shopping districts, where the audience is already in a buying frame of mind.

## Beyond the billboard: Metro Cash & Carry

![An oversized illuminated shopping-trolley float built for Metro Cash & Carry, on the road in Lahore](/media/work/metro-cash-carry-trolley.webp)

For its Electronics Gala in 2019, **Metro Cash & Carry** had an oversized, illuminated shopping trolley built and taken on the road. Not every outdoor idea is a flat face. This one was printed and fabricated by the same team that prints billboard faces.

## What these campaigns have in common

- **One message per face.** Delivery, a festival, a single product promise, a brand name.
- **Sites chosen for the audience.** Retail near shoppers, insurance on a residential corridor, a festival opposite the venue.
- **Static and digital used together** where the message needed to change.
- **Production and mounting by the same company**, from the flex print to the overnight mount.

All of these, and the sites they ran on, are on the [work page](/work) and the [network map](/network). To plan something similar, [how to plan an outdoor media campaign in Lahore](/blog/plan-outdoor-media-campaign-lahore) is the place to start.

<Callout title="A useful way to read these examples">

Look at each one and ask two questions: what is the single thing the board says, and who drives past it? Those two answers explain most good billboard campaigns.

</Callout>
