DHA is Lahore's highest-income catchment, and its billboards reach households that are hard to reach any other way: on DHA Phase 8 Commercial, Khayaban-e-Iqbal Road, the airport approach and the Ring Road towards the newer phases. Fangrove Media's DHA-area estate includes the largest face on its network, the <Stat name="largestFace" /> board in <Stat name="largestFaceArea" />, alongside billboards on the main feeder routes and shaped cutouts inside DHA and PHA.

This guide goes through each site serving DHA, what traffic it faces, and how they combine. For the rest of the city, see [where are the best billboard locations in Lahore](/blog/best-billboard-locations-lahore).

## Why advertise on billboards in DHA?

- **Audience.** DHA households are the core market for property, cars, private schools and hospitals, premium retail and financial services.
- **Few routes in.** Traffic into and around DHA funnels through a small number of boulevards and roundabouts, so a few well-placed faces reach most of it.
- **The airport.** Airport Road and the Ring Road carry business travellers and the DHA phases near the airport.

## The largest billboard in DHA Phase 8

<SiteTable area="DHA Phase 8" />

The DHA Phase 8 Commercial board at the Freeways 1st roundabout is the largest face in the estate. It faces traffic coming from the Ittehad roundabout on the DHA Phase 6 Boulevard, towards DHA Phase 5 Boulevard, with exposure to two roads. It is front-lit, so it works through the evening as well as the day. A face this size suits brand launches and property developments that want to be seen as the biggest thing on the road.

## Khayaban-e-Iqbal Road: into DHA and Packages Mall

<SiteTable area="DHA" />

The Khayaban-e-Iqbal Road face catches traffic coming from Walton Road towards DHA, Y Block and Packages Mall: a feeder route into DHA and a route to one of Lahore's largest malls in one placement.

## Airport Road and the Ring Road

<SiteTable area="Ring Road" />

The Airport Road face by the Garrison Golf Club faces traffic coming from the Hajj Terminal towards the airport, with a clean sightline. The Ring Road face catches traffic coming from Kala Shah Kaku towards DHA Phases 6 and 8 and the airport. The Ring Road is also a freight and long-haul corridor, which makes it a strong buy for B2B and automotive brands.

## Cutouts inside DHA and PHA

<SiteTable area="DHA / PHA" />

Inside the phases, Fangrove Media places shaped cutouts at major locations across the DHA and PHA areas. A cutout is a physical shape rather than a flat face, so it is used for launches that need a presence on the ground as well as on the main roads.

## How do advertisers combine DHA sites?

- **The DHA Phase 8 face plus the Ring Road**, for the newer phases and traffic coming from the north.
- **Khayaban-e-Iqbal plus Airport Road**, for the older phases and the airport.
- **Main roads plus cutouts**, for a launch that should be seen both on the way in and inside the phases.
- **DHA plus Gulberg**: the Jinnah Flyover in Gulberg faces traffic coming from DHA. [Billboard advertising in Gulberg](/blog/billboard-advertising-gulberg-lahore) covers it.

## What sizes are the DHA billboards?

They range from the largest face on the network in DHA Phase 8 to mid-sized faces on Khayaban-e-Iqbal Road, with landscape boards on Airport Road and the Ring Road, and small cutouts inside the phases. All the large faces here are front-lit. Exact dimensions are in the tables above, and [billboard sizes in Pakistan](/blog/billboard-sizes-pakistan) explains how they compare.

## How do you book a billboard in DHA?

Static faces are booked by the month, in cycles starting on the 1st or the 16th, with artwork due 72 hours before the overnight mount. Send the site (or just "DHA Phase 8" or "Airport Road"), the cycle you want and how many cycles, and a rate can usually come back the same day. [How to book a billboard in Lahore](/blog/how-to-book-a-billboard-lahore) walks through the process, and the [network map](/network) shows every site's photograph and approach.
