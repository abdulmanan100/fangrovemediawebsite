Gulberg is the commercial centre of Lahore, and its billboards sit on the roads that carry the city's office, shopping and dining traffic: Main Boulevard, Hussain Chowk, Kasoori Road, the Jinnah Flyover and MM Alam Road. Fangrove Media operates static billboards and the flagship LED screen across Gulberg and Gulberg III, each with its size, lighting, direction of approach and daily audience published.

This guide goes through each site in Gulberg, what traffic it faces, and how advertisers combine them. For the rest of the city, see [where are the best billboard locations in Lahore](/blog/best-billboard-locations-lahore).

## Why advertise on billboards in Gulberg?

Three reasons:

- **Commuter repetition.** Main Boulevard is one of the city's most-travelled commercial roads. The same offices empty onto the same roads twice a day.
- **Spending intent.** MM Alam Road is the fashion and dining strip, Liberty is a short drive away, and Kasoori Road leads to one of Lahore's busiest supermarkets. People here are already in a buying frame of mind.
- **Dwell time.** Hussain Chowk and the Main Boulevard approaches slow traffic down, so faces get a full read rather than a glance.

### Main Boulevard Gulberg

Two faces cover the boulevard in both directions. The Kalma Chowk approach is a portrait face on traffic coming from Kalma Chowk and the Liberty roundabout towards Jail Road; the Jail Road approach faces traffic coming from Jail Road and Cantt towards Kalma Chowk. Booked together, they cover both directions of the same commute.

### Hussain Chowk

Hussain Chowk carries three static faces and the large LED screen, all with two-road exposure. Faces A, B and C can be booked as a set to cover the whole roundabout as a single buy, and the LED screen lets a message change through the day. [SMD screen advertising in Lahore](/blog/smd-screen-advertising-lahore) covers how the screen is bought.

### Kasoori Road and the Jinnah Flyover

The Kasoori Road face stands opposite Al-Fatah, facing traffic coming from Hussain Chowk: retail intent at the door of one of the city's busiest supermarkets. The Jinnah Flyover face is an elevated landscape board facing traffic coming from DHA towards Firdous Market and Gulberg, with a long, unobstructed approach.

## How do advertisers combine Gulberg sites?

Common combinations on the Gulberg estate:

- **Both directions of Main Boulevard**, the Kalma Chowk and Jail Road approaches, for one commute covered twice a day.
- **The whole of Hussain Chowk**: Faces A, B and C as a set, with or without slots on the LED screen.
- **The retail circuit**: Kasoori Road opposite Al-Fatah plus the two MM Alam Road faces, for shoppers.
- **Gulberg plus DHA**: the Jinnah Flyover catches traffic coming from DHA, and pairs with the DHA sites in [billboard advertising in DHA Lahore](/blog/billboard-advertising-dha-lahore).

## What sizes are the Gulberg billboards?

The Gulberg estate mixes landscape and portrait faces, from mid-sized boards at Hussain Chowk to the large landscape face on the Jinnah Flyover and the Kasoori Road board. Every face here is front-lit, so it keeps working through the evening commute. Exact dimensions are in the tables above and on each site's page. Portrait and landscape faces need different layouts; [how to design a billboard that reads from the road](/blog/billboard-design-tips) explains why.

## How do you book a billboard in Gulberg?

Static faces are booked by the month, in cycles starting on the 1st or the 16th. Send the site (or just "Main Boulevard" or "MM Alam Road"), the cycle you want to start in and how many cycles, and a rate can usually come back the same day. [How to book a billboard in Lahore](/blog/how-to-book-a-billboard-lahore) covers the full process, and [what decides billboard rates](/blog/billboard-advertising-rates-lahore) explains the price.
