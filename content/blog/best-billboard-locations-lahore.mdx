There is no single best billboard location in Lahore. There is a best location *for a particular audience*. A board facing DHA traffic reaches a different city from one on Ferozepur Road, and a face read at walking pace inside a housing society does a different job from a bridge panel over the Canal. What the strong sites have in common is easier to pin down, and that is where to start.

## What makes a billboard location strong?

Four things, in roughly this order:

1. **Audience.** How many people pass the face each day. Every site on Fangrove Media's network publishes this figure.
2. **Speed and dwell time.** Slow traffic at roundabouts, signals and flyover approaches looks at a face for longer than fast traffic on an open road.
3. **Repetition.** Commuter routes carry the same people twice a day, five or six days a week. Outdoor works through frequency, and frequency depends on where the structure stands.
4. **Direction of approach.** A face is only seen by traffic driving towards it. A busy road is no use if the board faces the wrong way, which is why each site records its approach.

Lighting matters too. In winter a non-lit face misses the evening commute entirely. [What decides billboard rates](/blog/billboard-advertising-rates-lahore) covers how each of these shows up in the price.

## Canal Road and Mall Road

The Canal Road bridges and underpasses sit on one of the city's main commuter routes, linking Thokar Niaz Baig and Johar Town with Mall Road. Bridge and underpass panels are long, shallow faces read head-on by approaching traffic, and they carry some of the highest daily audience figures on the network. ## What about housing societies?

Inside gated societies, roadside billboards give way to smaller formats sold in runs: moppies in Bahria Town and cutouts across DHA and PHA. These reach residents at street level, close to home, and are bought for coverage of an area rather than for a single face. ## How do you choose between them?

Start from the audience rather than the map: where do the people you need to reach live, work and drive? Then shortlist faces on those routes, check each one's direction of approach, and compare published audience figures. The [network map](/network) filters every site by area and format, and each site page shows a photograph and a map pin. If you know the area but not the site, name the road when you enquire and we will suggest faces.
