Booking a billboard in Lahore is simpler than most first-time advertisers expect, and quicker. With an owned site, the same company handles the face, the print, the crew and the paperwork, so there is one conversation instead of four. This is the sequence Fangrove Media follows, from first enquiry to proof that the board is up.

## Step 1: Choose a site, or just a road

Start with the audience, not the board: where do the people you want to reach drive? Then look at the faces on those routes. Every site on the [network map](/network) publishes its photograph, size, lighting, direction of approach and daily audience, so you can shortlist before you call.

If you know the area but not the site, that is enough. Name the road or the neighbourhood and we will suggest faces. [Where are the best billboard locations in Lahore](/blog/best-billboard-locations-lahore) walks through the main corridors.

## Step 2: Pick a start date and a length

Static billboards in Lahore are booked by the month. On Fangrove Media's sites, cycles start on the **1st or the 16th**, and you choose how many cycles to take. One month and six months are priced differently.

## Step 4: Confirm, then supply artwork

Once the booking is confirmed, the artwork deadline is the one date to plan around: **72 hours before the mount**. Files are checked against the face's size and lighting before anything is printed, so a problem is caught while there is still time to fix it.

Build the file at 1:10 scale, in CMYK, at 150 dpi at that scale. The [star flex printing guide](/blog/star-flex-printing-guide) covers file setup and the common mistakes.

## Do you need a permit for a billboard in Lahore?

Outdoor advertising in Lahore is regulated. Structures and displays need approval from the PHA, and in cantonment areas from the cantonment authorities. On Fangrove Media's own sites that paperwork is handled end to end, so the advertiser never applies for it and never receives a takedown notice.

## How is booking a digital screen different?

The enquiry is the same; the purchase is different. On a [digital screen](/services/dooh) you book slots in a loop rather than a whole face, and the creative is a file rather than a print, so there is no printing or mounting step. Digital creative goes live within four working hours of approval, and you receive a weekly play log instead of a photograph. [DOOH vs static billboards](/blog/dooh-vs-static-billboards) compares the two.
